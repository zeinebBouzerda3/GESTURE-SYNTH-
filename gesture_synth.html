How to play 💜

1

Hold up your left hand (screen-left) and raise fingers. The count (1–7) picks a chord from the scale — 1 finger is the I chord, 4 fingers is the IV chord, and so on.

2

Move your left hand up or down to shift the chord an octave higher or lower.

3

Raise your right hand and move it up or down to open and close the filter — up brightens the sound, down darkens it.

4

Change the key and synth tone from the top-left menus any time.

5

Lower both hands to stop the sound.
